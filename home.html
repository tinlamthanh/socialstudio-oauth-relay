NoHypeAI Social Studio

Social Studio is a local desktop web app used by the NoHypeAI account owner to plan posts and view metrics for accounts they authorize. It runs on the owner's Mac, rather than as a public sign-up service.

The TikTok connection reads the authorized account's profile statistics and public video metrics. TikTok publishing is completed in TikTok's own app.
