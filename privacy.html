Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026. This policy describes NoHypeAI Social Studio, a local app used by the NoHypeAI account owner.

Data accessed

With your authorization, Social Studio reads account identifiers and profile details, follower counts, and post or video metrics from connected platforms. For TikTok, it requests basic profile information, account statistics, and the list and metrics of public videos. The app also stores the operator's plans, drafts, and scheduling records.

How data is used and stored

The data is used to show metrics and manage the operator's content workflow. Social Studio runs on the operator's Mac. Metrics and account records are stored locally in its database. Connection credentials are stored separately in the Mac's keychain or an encrypted local secret store. Social Studio does not sell connected account data.

Sharing and retention

Social Studio sends requests to the connected platforms to authenticate, read metrics, or perform authorized actions. If the operator enables an AI provider or media host, content needed for those optional features is sent to that provider or host. Local records remain until the operator removes them or deletes the app's local data. Revoking access in a platform's settings stops future authorized API access but does not automatically erase existing local records.

Choices and contact

You can revoke the connection in the platform's settings. For questions or a request to remove local account data, contact the operator on GitHub. Do not post passwords or access tokens in a public issue.
